# Literature review: What standard treatment strategies for colorectal cancer are recommended in the JSCCR Guidelines 2016?

Literature review on standard treatment strategies in the JSCCR Guidelines 2016

Colorectal cancer mortality in Japan was rising, surpassing 49,000 in 2015 [1], while many new treatment methods had been developed during recent decades [1].
In this context, the Japanese Society for Cancer of the Colon and Rectum Guidelines 2016 for the treatment of colorectal cancer were prepared to show standard treatment strategies for colorectal cancer [1].

The stated aims were to eliminate disparities among institutions in terms of treatment [1], to eliminate unnecessary treatment and insufficient treatment [1], and to deepen mutual understanding between health-care professionals and patients by making these Guidelines available to the general public [1].

The Guidelines were prepared by consensus reached by the JSCCR Guideline Committee [1], based on a careful review of the evidence retrieved by literature searches [1], and in view of the medical health insurance system and actual clinical practice settings in Japan [1].
They were intended for use as a tool for treating colorectal cancer in actual clinical practice settings [1], more specifically as a guide to obtaining informed consent from patients and choosing the method of treatment for each patient [1].

The abstracts provided do not specify the actual recommended modalities, indications, or algorithms contained in the JSCCR Guidelines 2016 [1]. Therefore, the specific standard treatment strategies cannot be determined from the papers provided [1].

Two related papers do not supply the missing 2016 treatment details. Paper [2] describes the JSCCR guidelines 2024 edition, prepared after deaths continued to rise to over 50,000 recorded in 2018 [2], with revisions to all aspects of treatment based on knowledge gained since the 2022 version for drug therapy and the 2019 version for other treatments [2]. Like the 2016 version, it was developed through consensus by the JSCCR Guideline Committee following evidence review and considering the insurance system and clinical practice in Japan [2].
Paper [3] addresses a distinct guideline for hereditary colorectal cancer, which accounts for less than 5% of all colorectal cancer cases [3]. It notes unique features including early age at onset, synchronous/metachronous occurrence, and association with multiple cancers in other organs necessitating different management from sporadic colorectal cancer [3], and highlights diagnostic challenges particularly for Lynch syndrome, once called hereditary non-polyposis colorectal cancer, whose concepts and diagnostic criteria have changed over time with continual research [3].

Open questions:
1. What stage-specific recommendations for endoscopy, surgery, radiotherapy, and systemic therapy were included in the full text of the JSCCR Guidelines 2016?
2. How did the 2016 Guidelines specify choosing the method of treatment for each patient and obtaining informed consent in Japanese practice settings?
3. What evidence review and consensus findings supported each specific standard treatment strategy in the 2016 Guidelines?
